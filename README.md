## 🎮 Presentation Controls
- **Next Slide:** Right Arrow, Down Arrow, Spacebar, PageDown, or on-screen `▶` / `Next` button
- **Previous Slide:** Left Arrow, Up Arrow, PageUp, or on-screen `◀` / `Previous` button
- **Fullscreen:** Press `F` or click the `Fullscreen` button
- **Jump to Slide:** Click on any of the dots at the bottom
- **Touch / Mobile:** Swipe left or right
- **Download PPTX:** Click the `Download PPT` button in the header
